Okada Manila and PhilWeb Launch Okada Play, Betting on the Digital-Luxury Convergence

Tiger Resort and PhilWeb Corp. officially roll out their jointly developed online gaming platform, marking one of the most prominent examples of a Philippine IR operator entering the domestic digital market with a local technology partner.

The Commercial Launch

PhilWeb Corp. and Tiger Resort, Leisure and Entertainment Inc. have officially launched Okada Play, an online gaming platform that brings the Okada Manila integrated resort brand into the Philippine digital gaming market. The commercial launch, confirmed through a joint announcement by both companies, represents a significant milestone in the convergence of land-based and online gaming in the Philippines.

The partnership agreement was signed by Nobuki Sato, Chief Operating Officer of Okada Manila, and Brian Ng, President of PhilWeb Corp., in a ceremony held at the Okada Manila property in Paranaque City. The signing formalized a development relationship that had been in progress for over a year, with Okada Play undergoing private beta testing with a limited player pool since early 2026.

"Okada Play is not simply an online casino attached to a resort name," Sato said during the launch event. "It is an extension of the experience that Okada Manila delivers on property. Our guests expect seamlessness, and this platform delivers it across channels."

The PhilWeb Technology Stack

Okada Play is powered by PhilWeb Corp.'s Online Gaming Platform (OGP) solution, a proprietary technology stack that PhilWeb has developed and refined over two decades in the Philippine gaming technology market. The OGP provides a full suite of capabilities: gaming content aggregation from over 40 licensed content suppliers, scalable cloud infrastructure, real-time analytics, payment processing (including GCash, Maya, and bank transfer integration), KYC/AML compliance modules, and 24/7 customer service support.

Brian Ng described the OGP as "the most battle-tested gaming platform in the Philippine market," noting that PhilWeb currently operates or licenses its technology to multiple PAGCOR-licensed gaming operators. "What makes the Okada Play deployment unique is the depth of the brand integration," Ng said. "We've built custom interfaces, VIP tier logic, and loyalty point conversion that connects directly to Okada Manila's on-property systems."

"Okada Play is not simply an online casino attached to a resort name. It is an extension of the experience that Okada Manila delivers on property."

Nobuki Sato, COO, Okada Manila

The Omnichannel Strategy

The launch of Okada Play is described by both companies as a central pillar of Okada Manila's broader digital transformation strategy. The concept of "omnichannel gaming," borrowed from the retail industry, refers to the seamless integration of physical and digital touchpoints. For Okada Manila, this means that a guest who plays at a baccarat table on the casino floor can continue playing from the same account on Okada Play after returning home, with loyalty points, VIP tier status, and promotional offers carrying across both channels.

This approach mirrors strategies that have been deployed by major operators in Las Vegas (notably MGM's BetMGM and Caesars Digital) and Macau (Sands China's digital loyalty programs). In the Philippine context, however, Okada Play represents one of the first attempts to implement a true omnichannel strategy by a major integrated resort.

Post-POGO Context: The Domestic Digital Pivot

The timing of the Okada Play launch is significant. The Philippines banned Philippine Offshore Gaming Operators (POGOs) in late 2024, eliminating a category of operators that had generated significant revenue but were plagued by controversy over criminal activity, tax evasion, and social disruption. In the wake of the POGO ban, PAGCOR created the PIGO license framework to regulate domestic-facing online gaming, effectively redirecting the industry's energy toward the Filipino player market.

Okada Play enters this landscape as a premium offering in a market that has been dominated by mass-market platforms like ArenaPlus (operated by DigiPlus Interactive) and a patchwork of e-Games licensees. The platform's differentiation strategy relies on the Okada Manila brand equity, the omnichannel loyalty integration, and PhilWeb's technological depth.

Notably, neither PhilWeb nor Tiger Resort disclosed specific GGR projections for Okada Play. When asked about revenue targets during the launch event, Ng deflected: "We are focused on building a sustainable platform with a world-class user experience. The revenue will follow the quality." This caution is consistent with the broader trend in the Philippine PIGO market, where operators have been reluctant to set public expectations for a licensing framework that is still maturing.

The Competitive Landscape

Okada Play enters a competitive field. ArenaPlus, operated by DigiPlus Interactive Corp. (PSE: PLUS), is the dominant PIGO platform in the Philippines, with a particularly strong position in sports betting. Solaire Online, launched by Bloomberry Resorts, offers a similar IR-to-digital model. And PhilWeb itself operates technology partnerships with multiple other PAGCOR licensees, raising questions about how the company manages potential channel conflict.

The platform's success will likely depend on three factors: the depth of the omnichannel integration (whether players actually convert from on-property to online and vice versa), the quality of the mobile experience (Philippine online gaming is overwhelmingly mobile-first, with over 80% of sessions originating from smartphones), and the effectiveness of the VIP retention program.

Financial Context

Okada Manila, operated by Tiger Resort, reported total revenue of PHP17.3 billion in 2025, making it one of the top three revenue-generating integrated resorts in the Philippines. The property, which includes over 800 hotel rooms, a casino floor with more than 500 gaming tables and 3,000 electronic gaming machines, and extensive non-gaming amenities, has been on a trajectory of recovery since resolving a protracted ownership dispute in 2023.

PhilWeb Corp., meanwhile, is one of the oldest gaming technology companies in the Philippines, with roots in the early-2000s e-Games era. The company's pivot from operating physical e-Games lounges to providing white-label and branded OGP solutions has positioned it as a key infrastructure player in the Philippine online gaming ecosystem.

"We've built custom interfaces, VIP tier logic, and loyalty point conversion that connects directly to Okada Manila's on-property systems."

Brian Ng, President, PhilWeb Corp.

Key Takeaways

  • Okada Play is a jointly developed online gaming platform by PhilWeb Corp. and Tiger Resort (Okada Manila), powered by PhilWeb's OGP technology.
  • The platform implements a true omnichannel strategy, linking online play to Okada Manila's on-property loyalty, VIP, and promotional systems.
  • No GGR projections were disclosed. The launch is positioned as a long-term brand extension rather than an immediate revenue driver.
  • Okada Play enters a competitive PIGO market dominated by ArenaPlus, with differentiation based on IR brand equity and omnichannel integration.
  • The launch reflects the broader post-POGO pivot toward domestic digital gaming in the Philippines.
